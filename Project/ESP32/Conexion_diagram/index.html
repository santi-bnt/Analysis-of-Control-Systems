Encoder signals: connect A and B to the GPIO inputs only if their output levels are safe for the ESP32. ESP32 GPIO inputs are 3.3 V logic.
Power: use the motor's 12 V supply through the BTS7960. Do not power the motor from the ESP32. Check the markings on your driver board for its logic supply and terminal names.
Pin assignments match Proyecto/ESP32/include/Config.h. Encoder scale in firmware: CPR = 1496 counts/revolution.
